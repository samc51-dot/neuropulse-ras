[ 01 / Research Abstract ]

Abstract: How Custom Beats Help the Body Move in Better Rhythm

The Problem: Neurologic Music Therapy uses audio beats to help people control their body movements by training the brain to match a rhythm. However, most therapies just use a basic, plain metronome click, which isn't always easy for the brain to follow.

What I Did: I built a computer program in Python that creates custom music tracks with special drum accents and clearer beats. Then, I tested 25 people by having them tap along to both a standard metronome click and my custom music tracks to measure their timing accuracy in milliseconds.

What I Found: People kept much better rhythm when listening to the custom music tracks. Their tapping timing errors dropped by 18.4% compared to the basic metronome.

Why It Matters: Adding clearer musical beats makes it much easier for the brain and body to stay on rhythm, opening up better, low-cost digital tools for movement therapy.

Primary Outcome: 18.4% Reduction in Tapping Timing Error

Therapeutic Framework

  • Parametric Signal Generation: Custom Python audio processing pipeline generating frequency-accented rhythms.
