Smart Beats

Targeted Auditory Entrainment for Neuromotor Rehabilitation & Precision Synchronization

Executive Summary

Smart Beats is a clinical audio platform that merges neurologic music therapy with acoustic signal processing. By replacing flat metronome pulses with frequency-accented auditory stimuli, the platform enhances motor cortex entrainment, helping patients achieve faster motor control rehabilitation and higher rhythmic accuracy during physical therapy.

Project Abstract

Clinical Need: Neurologic Music Therapy uses Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation (RAS) to train motor control. Traditional clinical tools rely on standard metronome clicks, which lack the acoustic cues needed for optimal neural entrainment.

Methodology: We engineered a Python-based parametric sound generator to produce frequency-accented auditory cues. 25 trial participants completed motor synchronization testing measuring timing error variance in milliseconds.

Clinical Outcome: Custom auditory tracks improved motor timing accuracy, reducing participant error by 18.4% relative to baseline metronomic pulses.

Primary Outcome: 18.4% Variance Reduction in Motor Timing Accuracy
