[ 01 / Research Abstract ]

Abstract: How Custom Beats Help the Body Move in Better Rhythm

The Problem: Neurologic Music Therapy uses audio beats to help people control their body movements by training the brain to match a rhythm. However, most therapies just use a basic metronome click.

What I Did: I built a computer program in Python that creates custom music tracks with special drum accents and tested 25 people tapping along to measure timing accuracy.

What I Found: People kept much better rhythm when listening to custom music tracks. Timing errors dropped by 18.4% compared to a basic metronome.

Why It Matters: Adding clearer musical beats makes it easier for the brain and body to stay on rhythm, opening up low cost digital tools for movement therapy.

Primary Outcome: 18.4% Reduction in Tapping Timing Error

[ 06 / Rhythmic Prediction ]

Phase Resetting & Internal Clock Test

What It Is: Measures your brain's internal rhythm generator. A steady beat plays to entrain your motor system, then suddenly cuts silent for 3 full beats. You must keep tapping in exact tempo through the silence until the audio returns.

What It Helps With: Evaluating internal motor clock accuracy and predictive timing without relying on constant external cues: critical for gait stability in Parkinson's disease and post stroke movement recovery.

01 Start Audio Pace

Click "Start Internal Clock Test". Match your taps to the audible beat (500ms tempo).

02 Keep Tapping In Silence

When the audio cuts out, do not stop. Continue tapping at the exact same rhythm.

[ 07 / Inhibitory Control ]

Off Beat Motor Suppression Test

What It Is: Evaluates inhibitory motor control under rhythmic disruption. A main accent pulse plays every 600ms that you must tap along with. Random high pitched syncopated "off beats" will attempt to distract your rhythm.

What It Helps With: Assessing your brain's ability to suppress unwanted motor reflexes and ignore environmental audio noise: a vital cognitive motor skill for maintaining steady balance and gait focus.

01 Lock to Low Accent

Click "Start Suppression Test". Tap the trigger ONLY when you hear the heavy low pitch beat.

02 Ignore Off Beats

Do NOT tap when high pitched syncopated distractor pulses fire between main beats.

03 Measure Suppression

See your final accuracy score based on correct main beat taps minus false off beat reactions.
