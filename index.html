Smart Beats

Using Custom Music Rhythms to Help the Brain and Body Move Better

Project Overview

Started in August 2025, this project explores how custom music rhythms help people keep better movement timing compared to standard metronome clicks. It combines principles from Neurologic Music Therapy (NMT) and simple computer programming.

Key Result: Custom music tracks reduced timing errors by 18.4%

How It Works

  • Parametric Audio: Custom Python scripts generate rhythms with micro-adjusted tempos and drum accents.
  • Rhythmic Entrainment: The brain aligns motor movements to strong, predictable musical beats.
  • Targeted Testing: Tested on 25 participants measuring timing accuracy in milliseconds.
